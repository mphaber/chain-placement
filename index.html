How to play

Tap an empty cell to place the tile shown under Place next. A tile merges only when it touches at least 2 tiles of the same value directly up, down, left or right (never diagonally): those neighbours are consumed and the tile doubles, staying in its cell. If the doubled tile now touches 2 or more of its new value, it merges again, and again, forming a chain. A merge scores the tile created × its chain step: the first merge in a turn is ×1, the second ×2, the third ×3, and so on, so deep chains are where the points are. Tiles are dealt from a shuffled bag of ten (seven 2s, two 4s, one 8), and the game ends when the board is completely full. Dashed outlines mark cells where placing now would merge.
