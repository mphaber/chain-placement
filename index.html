How to play

1 · Place

Tap an empty square to place the tile shown. Tiles come from a bag of 10 (mostly 2s, some 4s, one 8). You can always see the next 3. Stuck with a bad order? Shuffle reorders the tiles still in the bag: you start with 1 shuffle and earn another each time you finish a bag, up to 2.

2 · Merge

A tile merges when it joins a connected group of 3 or more tiles of the same number, counting the tile you placed. Connections run up, down, left or right; diagonals do not count. Drop it between two singles to join them, or next to a pair to complete it. The whole group collapses into your tile and it doubles. A group of 1 or 2 just sits there: bait for a trap. Big tiles are unstable: at 64 and up, a connected pair is enough to merge, which is how chains keep rolling at the top end.

3 · Chain & score

If the new tile is now part of a connected group of 3+ of its new number, it merges again: a chain. Each merge scores the tile created × its step in the chain: 1st merge ×1, 2nd ×2, 3rd ×3.

2s make a 4 (4 pts) → that 4 makes an 8 (8 × 2 = 16 pts) → that 8 makes a 16 (16 × 3 = 48 pts). One tap: 68 points.

The game ends when the board is full. In Daily, everyone gets the same tiles that day.

Free Play deals fresh bags every game. Daily deals everyone the same tile order for the date shown, and replays are allowed while we prototype.
